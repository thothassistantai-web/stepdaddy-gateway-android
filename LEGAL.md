## What this software is

StepDaddy Gateway is a **local IPTV gateway / aggregator**. It:

- Runs on your own device (Android TV stick, phone, or emulator)
- Fetches **public metadata and stream URLs** from third-party websites you configure
- Serves M3U playlists and XMLTV EPG to **local** IPTV clients (e.g. TiviMate) on the same device or LAN
